# Engineer, experimenter, persistent question-asker.

I’m John Goalby, a software engineer who likes turning awkward, repetitive, or poorly understood problems into useful software.

My work has crossed web development, automation, developer tooling, and generative AI. I’m happiest when I can understand how people actually work, find the friction in a process, and build something that makes the next attempt easier.

## How I approach the work

- **Start with the real problem.** The first description of a problem is rarely the whole story.
- **Make the useful path obvious.** Good engineering and good product design reinforce each other.
- **Respect constraints.** Limited tools, unusual devices, security requirements, and legacy systems often produce the most interesting decisions.
- **Keep learning in public.** Writing clarifies what I know and exposes what I still need to understand.

That last point is why this site includes both polished technical guides and less formal observations. They are all pages from the same notebook.

## What I’m exploring now

I’m improving [log2div](/work/log2div/), experimenting with practical applications of language models, and developing new projects that combine engineering with product thinking.

I also remain unusually interested in making an iPad a viable development environment. Constraints can be frustrating, but they are excellent teachers.

> Build something useful. Observe what happens. Improve the next version.
